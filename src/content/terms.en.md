## Your data

Your household's apartments, ratings, comments, locations and PDFs are encrypted in your browser before they are stored. We cannot read them, and we cannot restore them if you lose your passphrase and your recovery code. Any member can download a full copy from the Household page; keep one.

What the server does hold, and why:

- **Your account**: your email address, your name, your language, and, if you sign in with Google, the link to your Google profile picture and the id Google gives your account. Needed to sign you in and to show your name to your household.
- **Your household**: its name, who is a member, pending invitations (with the invited address), and the encryption keys, which are themselves encrypted.
- **Usage counts**: how many PDFs, addresses and listing links each household has processed, and what each credit paid for. Per household, never per person, and never what was processed. We review them to check use against what was paid for.
- **Payments**: which household bought how many credits and when. Card details stay with Stripe.
- **Beta requests**: the email address you gave on the landing page, to send you an invitation.

When you read a PDF, place an address or check a listing link, that one PDF, address or link is sent through our server to the service that does the work, held in memory and not stored. The landing page's privacy section names each of them.

## Who processes data for us

- Vercel hosts the app.
- Turso stores the database, in the EU (Ireland).

- Google provides sign-in, addresses and travel times.
- An AI model provider reads PDFs, under zero data retention.

- Stripe takes payments.

Some of them process data outside Switzerland, in the EU and the United States.
