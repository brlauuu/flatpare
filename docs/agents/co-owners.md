# Co-owners (#192)

Full reference. The rules for this area are in `.claude/rules/co-owners.md` (loaded when working on its files), with anything cross-cutting in AGENTS.md; this file holds the detail, the measurements and the history behind them.

- **A household has one or more owners, all equal.** Any owner may invite, remove (other owners too), rotate the data key, regenerate the recovery kit, see Recompute, and promote or demote: `PATCH /api/household/members/:userId` with `{ role }` → `setMemberRole` (`src/lib/household.ts`). An owner may step down, leave, or be removed while **another owner remains**; the last one cannot (409 on demote/remove, 400 on leave). Changing your own role refreshes your session (`unstable_update`) and the page calls `router.refresh()`; everyone else's token keeps its old role, which nothing reads for a decision.
- **"At least one owner" is a condition inside the statement that makes the change** (`moreThanOneOwner` / `isOwner` subqueries), not a count read first, so two owners demoting or removing each other at the same moment leave exactly one. The concurrency test in `src/lib/__tests__/household.test.ts` mixes all three operations from both sides. `setMemberRole` is one guarded `UPDATE` but **runs in `db.transaction` on purpose**: it runs beside leave/remove transactions, and on the local file only `db.transaction` waits out `SQLITE_BUSY` (#225) — as a bare statement it failed under that test.
- **`households.owner_id` is the creator, not "the owner"**, and is read by nothing. Every owner check reads `household_members.role` from the database.
- **One recovery kit per household**, so whoever makes a new one (an owner regenerating or rotating, a member recovering with the code) voids every other copy. Every kit path records `recovery_created_by` (`recoveryColumns(r, byUserId)` in `src/lib/member-keys.ts`), and each *other* owner sees "X made a new recovery kit on <date>" on the Household page until they dismiss it (`POST /api/household/recovery-notice`); promotion clears the new owner's dismissal so they see it too. The notice travels with `GET /api/household/members` as `recoveryNotice`.
- **Any member holding the data key can wrap it for a pending member** (`fulfilWraps` is not owner-gated), so an absent owner never strands a new member; `member-keys.test.ts` pins it.
- Decided on the issue (2026-10-03) and built early, before 1.0.0, at the owner's request (2026-10-23).
